# VoltWay
Turning road heat into useful electricity.

Roads absorb a lot of heat from the sun, especially here in the UAE. I wanted to see whether some of that heat could be put to use, so I built VoltWay.

## How it works
VoltWay uses a thermoelectric generator to produce electricity from a temperature difference. The road system I’m proposing uses hot pavement on one side and a heat-transfer path toward cooler ground on the other.

For my prototype, I used a heating pack, a thermoelectric module and a heat sink to test the idea.

## What I worked on
I researched the science, designed, built and tested the prototype, and documented the project. I also made a presentation, poster and video, then refined the project with advice from mentors.

## What’s next
I want to improve the setup and explore how it performs outdoors, where temperatures and cooling conditions change throughout the day.
